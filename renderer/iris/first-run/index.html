Welcome to GemAir Assist

GemAir installs apps for you and answers questions about what's on your screen. Install guides work right away, offline. To have GemAir answer you, it needs to reach a model — and the models it uses are free.

Use the free models

No account, no card, no key. GemAir talks to opencode.ai's free models.

GemAir will send your question — and, when you ask about your screen, a screenshot — to opencode.ai to be answered. Nothing is stored there by GemAir, and no paid model is ever requested.

Paste your own OpenCode key

Optional. A key of your own raises your rate limit. Still free models only.

Use the opencode CLI on this computer

Runs through the opencode binary you already have, with whatever it is signed in to. GemAir never sees that sign-in.
